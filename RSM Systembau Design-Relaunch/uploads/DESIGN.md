# RSM Systembau GmbH | Digital Infrastructure Design System
**Project Codename:** Enterprise Nexus 2025

## 1. Design Philosophy
"Industrial Premium meets Zero-Risk Trust". Absolute authority, engineering precision, and financial solidity.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary:** #0F172A (Deep Slate / Industrial Navy)
- **Backgrounds:** #F8FAFC (Architectural Off-White), #FFFFFF (White)
- **Accent:** #EA580C (Safety Orange)
- **Technical:** #64748B (BIM Gray)

## 3. Typography
- **Headlines:** Space Grotesk (Bold, uppercase for nav)
- **Body:** Inter (Clean, highly legible)
- **Technical/Data:** JetBrains Mono (Monospace for engineering specs)

## 4. Components & Layout
- **Sticky Header:** Minimalist, high contrast.
- **Hero Sections:** Full-width, darkened imagery, massive typography.
- **Bento Grids:** 3-column asymmetric layouts for services.
- **Footer:** Massive, structured, and legally secure.
- **Cards:** Sharp corners (no rounding or very minimal 2px), subtle borders.

## 5. Spacing System
- Precise 8px grid system. 
- Generous whitespace (128px+ section padding) to emphasize "Premium" positioning.
