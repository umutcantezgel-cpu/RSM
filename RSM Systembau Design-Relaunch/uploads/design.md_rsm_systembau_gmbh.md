# RSM Systembau GmbH | Digital Infrastructure Design System
**Project Codename:** Enterprise Nexus 2025
**Authority:** Principal Enterprise Web Architect / Lead UI/UX Director

---

## 1. Design Philosophy: "Industrial Premium meets Zero-Risk Trust"
The visual language must command the same respect as a 7-figure construction site. Every pixel must communicate engineering precision, financial stability, and absolute compliance.

*   **Engineering Rigor:** Strict grid alignment, mathematical spacing, and brutalist typography.
*   **Zero-Risk Trust:** Clean layouts, high-contrast readability, and a complete absence of "web fluff."
*   **Digital Dominance:** Modern Next.js 15 Jamstack architecture to contrast with legacy competitors.

---

## 2. Color System (Tailwind CSS v4)
*   **Primary (Authority):** `Deep Slate / Industrial Navy (#0F172A)`
    *   Used for: Header, Hero backgrounds, Footer, and core UI elements.
*   **Background (Canvas):** `Architectural Off-White (#F8FAFC)` & `Pure White (#FFFFFF)`
    *   Used for: Content sections and massive whitespace to ensure breathing room.
*   **Accent/Action (Precision):** `Safety Orange (#EA580C)`
    *   Used for: High-impact CTAs, status indicators, and critical navigational highlights.
*   **Functional (Technical):** `BIM Gray (#64748B)`
    *   Used for: Technical data, metadata, and borders.

---

## 3. Typography
*   **Headlines:** `Space Grotesk` (or Monument Extended). 
    *   *Characteristics:* Sharp, geometric, brutalist. High-impact uppercase for navigation.
*   **Body Copy:** `Inter`. 
    *   *Characteristics:* Maximum legibility at all weights. Clean, neutral, professional.
*   **Technical Data:** `JetBrains Mono`. 
    *   *Characteristics:* Used for BIM data, project specifications, and ESG metrics to emphasize the "PropTech" edge.

---

## 4. Layout & Components (Wireframe Analysis)
*   **Header:** Sticky, minimalist, high-contrast. Rapid access to the 4 core clusters.
*   **Hero Sections:** Full-width, heavy weight. Darkened architectural video backgrounds with crisp, centered typography.
*   **Bento Grids:** 3-column asymmetric layouts for service overviews. 
    *   *Logic:* One large "Anchor" card (Featured Service) followed by smaller "Detail" cards.
*   **Footer:** Massive, structured, and legally ironclad. Separate sectors for Corporate, Services, Career, and Compliance.
*   **Zero-Trust Portal:** A dedicated, ultra-secure login UI for the employee portal, replacing the insecure PDF-based methods of the past.
