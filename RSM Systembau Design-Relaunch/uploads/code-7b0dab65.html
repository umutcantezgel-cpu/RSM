The EWS Frankfurt facility required a foundational surface capable of withstanding extreme mechanical loads while maintaining absolute dimensional stability for automated logistics equipment. This project highlights our capacity to execute massive-scale, high-precision flooring systems under strict enterprise timelines.

01 / Challenge

Engineering high-durability surfaces

Heavy logistics machinery imposes relentless stress on floor surfaces. The primary challenge was engineering a sub-floor and tiling system that could endure constant point-loading from heavy-duty forklifts and automated guided vehicles (AGVs) without compromising structural integrity or levelness over a 1,200sqm expanse.

02 / Solution

Large-Format Porcelain Stoneware

To meet the stringent load requirements, we specified industrial-grade, large-format porcelain stoneware. The critical path involved high-precision jointing using advanced epoxy grouts to create a near-seamless, monolith-like surface that minimizes vibration for sensitive logistics equipment.

MATERIAL STRENGTH > 50 N/mm²
JOINT TOLERANCE ± 0.5 mm
03 / Integration

PropTech Driven Execution

The sheer scale dictated a digital-first approach. We implemented a fully integrated Building Information Modeling (BIM) workflow. This allowed for exact material quantification, automated clash detection in sub-floor heating elements, and orchestration of Just-in-Time material deliveries, eliminating site congestion.
